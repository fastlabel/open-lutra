## Uses Permitted Without Prior Consultation (Nominative Use)

The following uses are permitted without prior consultation, to the extent they accurately convey factual information.

* **References to OpenLUTRA**: Statements such as "uses OpenLUTRA," "compatible with OpenLUTRA," or "developed based on OpenLUTRA"
* **References in academic papers, technical articles, blog posts, and the like**: Use of "OpenLUTRA" as the formal name of the software in explanatory text
* **Use of the name for search engine optimization or categorization**

When making such uses, please observe the following:

* Spell the name accurately as "OpenLUTRA." As far as possible, please avoid variant spellings such as "Open LUTRA" (with a space), "openlutra," or "OpenLutra," and please avoid using the repository name `open-lutra` as a product name
* Do not suggest any unwarranted affiliation with, endorsement by, or sponsorship by FastLabel or its products

## Uses Requiring Prior Consultation

For the following uses, please consult FastLabel in advance to avoid confusion or mistaken identification.

* **Use of "OpenLUTRA" / "FastLabel" in product names, service names, company names, domain names, social media account names, and the like**: For example, using names such as "OpenLUTRA Pro," "OpenLUTRA Cloud," or "FastLabel Robotics" as the name of your own product
* **Creation of advertising or promotional materials that prominently feature FastLabel's trademarks**
* **Promotional uses that go beyond the scope of a compatibility statement** (e.g., large-scale marketing activities that prominently feature "Powered by OpenLUTRA")

## Guidelines for Forks

You are free to fork and modify this project in accordance with the Apache License 2.0. With respect to derivative works, please observe the following.

1. **Change of name**: If you distribute a fork as an independent, separate product, please give it a different name that does not include "OpenLUTRA"
2. **Reference to the original**: You may describe your fork as "a fork of OpenLUTRA" or "Based on OpenLUTRA" without prior consultation
3. **Retention of the NOTICE file**: In accordance with the requirements of Section 4(d) of the Apache License 2.0, please retain the relevant entries in the NOTICE file
4. **Avoidance of confusion**: Please refrain from any representation suggesting that the fork is an official release of, or is officially supported by, FastLabel

## Use of "Powered by" / "Compatible with"

The following uses are permitted without prior consultation:

* Including a modest "Powered by OpenLUTRA" notice on websites, in documentation, or in README files
* Listing "OpenLUTRA" as part of a technology stack description in settings screens, About screens, and the like

The following uses require prior consultation:

* Use of "Powered by OpenLUTRA" as a central element of product packaging or advertising
